Terms of use

Fenrir Bridge by Frisky provides administrative software for creating stable public access links that route to private operational destinations, including Telegram groups, live rooms, payment links, documents, booking links, and support links.

By using the service, you confirm that you are authorized to administer the domains, groups, links, and destinations you connect. You are responsible for the accuracy, legality, permissions, and safety of any target link you configure.

Fenrir may suspend access to links, workspaces, or accounts that create security risk, abuse, fraud, spam, impersonation, payment disputes, or legal exposure.

Acceptable use

You may not use Fenrir to distribute malware, phishing, deceptive links, unlawful content, harassment, spam, unauthorized access, stolen credentials, or content that violates platform rules for Telegram, Stripe, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft, Apple, or other connected providers.

Admins must rotate or revoke compromised invites promptly. Public Fenrir links must not be used to misrepresent ownership of a brand, organization, domain, community, or payment destination.

Payments & refunds

Paid plans are subscription products unless otherwise stated at checkout. Plan limits may include domains, active links, live rooms, audit retention, team seats, and concierge support.

Refunds are handled case by case for billing errors, duplicate charges, or service failures. Domain purchases, third-party provider fees, and partner/affiliate purchases may be subject to the terms of the external provider.

Contact

For legal, abuse, billing, privacy, or security requests, contact Frisky Developments through Frisky Signal or use the configured support channel for your workspace.
