What MyFenrir actually is

MyFenrir (the app at myfenrir.com) is the control room for Fenrir Bridge, a product by Frisky Developments. Fenrir is named for the wolf — a guardian that sits in front of your community and decides who gets through.

The problem Fenrir solves is simple. When you run a private Telegram group, a paid course, a VIP room, or a client community, you usually share a raw Telegram invite link. That link is fragile: it gets forwarded, screenshotted, and leaked, and once it's out you either live with strangers walking in or you burn the link and re-send it to everyone.

Fenrir Bridge replaces that raw link with one stable, branded URL — something like customer.myfenrir.com/main or join.your-domain.com/main. That public URL never changes. Behind it, Fenrir holds the real Telegram invite. If an invite leaks, you rotate or revoke it in seconds and the public URL keeps working for everyone who's supposed to be there. That behind-the-scenes swap is the invite kill-switch.

Link armor

One clean public link stands in for messy private invites. Your brand on the door, never a raw t.me URL.

Invite kill-switch

Leaked invite? Rotate or revoke the underlying Telegram invite. The public URL stays the same for real members.

Community Gate

A branded front door that verifies identity, applies your rules, then routes approved people to the private destination.

Who it's for#

Fenrir is built for Telegram group admins, creators, operators, and small businesses — anyone who runs paid or private access and is tired of leaky links. The same tools cover a single hobby group and a multi-client agency.

  • Members — people who want into a community. You arrive at a gate, prove who you are, and get routed to the real room.
  • Community owners — admins who set up the gate, connect a Telegram group, brand the door, and control invites, moderation, and billing.

Two products, one wolf

You'll see the names MyFenrir, Fenrir Bridge, Fenrir Protocol, and Telegram Lock used together. They're facets of the same system: MyFenrir is the site, Fenrir Bridge is the product, Fenrir Protocol is the access engine, and a Telegram Lock is one guarded link.

Vocabulary

The language of Fenrir

Fenrir uses its own operational words. Learn these six and the rest of the wiki reads easily.

Telegram Lock

One guarded link. The public URL is stable; Fenrir rotates the Telegram invite behind it. Your plan sets how many Locks you can run.

Fenrir Bridge

The routing layer between a public link and a private destination — it checks identity, payment state, and revocation rules before letting anyone through.

Community Gate

A branded public page (e.g. /community/your-name) where visitors prove who they are and your rules decide access.

Invite kill-switch

The action of rotating or revoking the raw Telegram invite. Kills a leaked invite without changing the public URL.

Member vault

The isolated record of a community's membership, invite status, review state, and audit history — kept separate per community.

Fenrir Protocol

The overall access engine: domains + SSL + Telegram readiness + invite control + audit, shown in the app as a living "protocol" system.

For members

Signing in with your FriskyDev account

MyFenrir uses a single Frisky account (the FriskyDev identity) for signing in. There's no separate password to create — you sign in through a trusted provider and Fenrir issues you a Frisky user and workspace.

How sign-in works#

1

Open the app or a gate

Go to myfenrir.com, or open a community's Fenrir link. Choose to sign in.

2

Pick a provider

Continue with Apple, Google, or Microsoft. "More sign-in options" opens the AuthKit picker for additional methods.

3

Come back authenticated

Sign-in happens on login.myfenrir.com and returns you to the app with a session. Fenrir maps you to a Frisky user ID and org ID.

4

(Optional) Save a passkey

After OAuth sign-in you can register a passkey on your device for faster, passwordless return sign-in.

What identity you get

Every signed-in person receives a Frisky user ID (like frisky_usr_…) and an org ID (like frisky_org_…). These are how Fenrir tracks your workspace, plan, and entitlements — the same identity powers the app and any gate you own.

Known behavior: shared session on the live app today

On the live site right now, the workspace can open already showing a signed-in Frisky account because sessions are held in a shared cookie across .myfenrir.com. If you land in a session that isn't yours, or sign-out returns you to the same one, that's the known shared-session behavior — not a lost account. See Troubleshooting → Login loop for exactly what works today.

The Community Gate flow

When you open a community's Fenrir link, you don't see a raw Telegram, Zoom, or payment URL — you see a branded gate. The gate runs a short, consistent flow: Verify → Rules → Enter.

01 · Verify
Prove identity

You confirm who you are, usually via a magic link sent to your email. No password to remember.

→
02 · Rules
Rules check

The gate applies the owner's entry mode and rules — open, review-first, invite-only, or closed.

→
03 · Enter
Approve & route

Approved members are routed to the real private destination. Blocked or pending visitors stop here.

Verify — proving who you are#

The default login method for a gate is a magic link. You enter your email, Fenrir sends a one-time link, and clicking it confirms your identity. Some gates also allow the same trusted providers used across MyFenrir. The point is the same: the gate needs to know you're a real, identifiable person before applying rules.

Rules — how the door decides#

What happens after you verify depends entirely on the owner's entry mode:

Entry modeWhat it means for you
OpenVerify and you're in immediately.
Review-firstYou request access; the owner reviews and approves you. You may wait a bit.
Invite-onlyYou need a valid invite code or to be on the allowlist to proceed.
ClosedThe gate isn't accepting new members right now.

Enter — reaching the real room#

Once the rules approve you, Fenrir routes you to the private destination — a Telegram group, a live room, or another stable link the owner has set. Every decision (approved, in review, blocked) is written to the community's audit trail, so access is accountable on both sides.

Why the raw link stays hidden

You only ever see the branded public URL. The private Telegram invite or room link stays behind the Bridge, so it can't be copied out of a screenshot — and if it ever leaks, the owner rotates it without disturbing your access.

Signing in creates your account. Activation is the separate step that turns a free workspace into a paid, provisioned one — and links your Telegram identity so Fenrir can act on your behalf.

The activation flow#

Fenrir describes activation as a clean pipeline: Fenrir guides, Stars confirms, access unlocks.

1

Fenrir concierge qualifies you

The in-app assistant explains setup, DNS, Telegram, and which plan fits — before you pay anything.

2

OAuth account

Google, Microsoft, or Apple sign-in creates your Frisky user and org IDs and protects the workspace.

3

Telegram Stars confirms payment

The official Telegram Stars payment box handles the transaction. Fenrir never asks for card details for this rail.

4

Backend entitlement

Once Telegram confirms payment, Fenrir flips your entitlement to active and turns on domains, Locks, rooms, and audit scope.

5

Provisioning

Cloudflare and Telegram jobs create the real operating link, and your workspace reflects the new limits.

Link your Telegram ID#

A key part of activation is connecting your Telegram account to your Frisky ID. In the app this is the "Link Telegram ID" action — it opens a handoff to the Fenrir bot. You send the bot a one-time /start link_… code, and Fenrir binds that Telegram account to your workspace so Locks and invite actions run against the right identity.

Payment truth lives in the backend

Fenrir never claims access is active until the backend entitlement says so. The in-app "Beta preview" role/plan switchers are visual only — real capacity always comes from confirmed activation. Stripe card billing is signposted as a future standby; Telegram Stars is today's live rail.

Rules & roles#

For community owners

Locks, domains, rooms & links

The gate is the front door; these are the rooms behind it. This is the operational core of Fenrir Bridge.

Telegram Locks & invite rotation#

A Telegram Lock pairs a stable public slug with a Telegram group. You create one by choosing a verified domain, a slug (e.g. main), and the Telegram chat ID + group name. From then on:

  • The lock URL stays stable — share it freely.
  • Fenrir rotates the Telegram invite behind it whenever you need.
  • If an invite leaks, you rotate or revoke it; the public URL is unaffected and real members keep their access.

Cloudflare DNS Wizard#

To use your own domain, the DNS Wizard walks you through pointing DNS to Cloudflare so Lock URLs get certificates automatically. You keep any registrar — Porkbun, Dynadot, GoDaddy, Namecheap — Fenrir only needs DNS routed through Cloudflare. The two records it uses:

# Prove domain ownership
Type: TXT    Name: _fenrir    Value: fenrir-verify=<token>    TTL: Auto

# Route your subdomain to Fenrir
Type: CNAME  Name: join       Value: bridge.myfenrir.com     TTL: Auto

No domain yet? Start instantly on a managed Fenrir subdomain like customer.myfenrir.com, then upgrade to a custom domain when you're ready. A live domain search (RDAP + DNS) helps you find a clean front door before wiring DNS.

Cloudflare Live Rooms Starter+#

Live Rooms route a video call through Fenrir first, so you share a branded room link instead of a raw Zoom/Meet URL. Providers include Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Whereby, or a custom room. This is a paid feature from Starter and above — Free keeps a single Telegram Lock. Starting at Pro, you can add your logo and branded room visuals.

The All Links vault#

Beyond Telegram, the All Links vault tracks payment links, docs, booking links, support links, and live-room links in one place. You choose what to share or hide — a shareable vault includes only the stable URLs you select, never raw invites.

Accounts & privacy

Accounts & login

MyFenrir runs on the shared FriskyDev account model: one identity, issued through trusted providers, that follows you across the app and any gate you own.

The shared FriskyDev account model#

When you sign in, Fenrir doesn't store a password. It authenticates you through a provider (Google, Microsoft, Apple, or AuthKit) and issues a Frisky user ID and org ID. That pair is your portable identity — the thing plans, entitlements, Locks, and gates attach to.

  • One account, many surfaces. The same Frisky identity powers the dashboard, your Telegram Locks, and the Community Gates you own.
  • Provider-backed. Sign-in happens on login.myfenrir.com; you return with a session cookie scoped to .myfenrir.com.
  • Passkeys. After OAuth sign-in you can register a passkey for faster passwordless return.

How portability works#

Because your identity is a Frisky user + org ID rather than a per-community login, it's portable:

Your Telegram identity binds to you

Linking your Telegram ID connects that Telegram account to your Frisky ID — so if you move between workspaces, the binding travels with your identity, not a single group.

Communities stay isolated

Membership records live in each community's own vault. Portability of identity doesn't mix one community's member data with another's.

Keep any registrar

Domain portability too: bring any registrar, route DNS through Cloudflare, and your Locks and gates ride on top. You never transfer your domain to Fenrir.

Re-link is always possible

If a Telegram link code expires, generate a fresh one from MyFenrir — the bind re-attaches to the same Frisky identity.

Accounts & privacy

Privacy & what stays hidden

Fenrir's whole design is about not exposing sensitive links and keeping member data separated.

Troubleshooting

Straight answers about the things people actually hit — including what's genuinely working on the live app today.

Login loop / "already signed in as someone else"#

Known issue, documented honestly

The live app currently keeps its session in a cookie shared across .myfenrir.com. As a result the workspace can open already signed in, and signing out can return you to the same shared session instead of a clean login. This is a known shared-session quirk, not your account being lost.

What works today:

  • OAuth sign-in itself works. Continuing with Google, Microsoft, or Apple on login.myfenrir.com completes and returns you to the app.
  • Use the reliable identity anchor. The most dependable way to bind your identity is Link Telegram ID → complete the handoff in the Fenrir bot. That connects your Telegram account to your Frisky ID directly.
  • Judge status by the backend, not the header. Plan and role in the "Beta preview" switchers are visual only. Trust the Frisky Account panel and confirmed activation for your real entitlement.
  • If you're stuck in a shared session, try a private/incognito window (no pre-existing .myfenrir.com cookie) and sign in fresh there.

We keep this section truthful on purpose: we won't promise a login flow that isn't fully solid yet. If OAuth returns an error, the URL usually carries an auth_error=… hint (e.g. missing_code, exchange_failed) — include it when you ask for help.

I can't get through a gate#

  • Check the entry mode. If the gate is review-first, you're waiting on the owner. If invite-only, you need a valid code or to be allowlisted. If closed, it isn't accepting members right now.
  • Finish the magic link. Verification usually needs you to click a one-time link in your email. Check spam and use the most recent link.
  • Owner editing a gate? "Not an owner or allowlisted admin" means you're signed in with the wrong identity for that gate — use the owning account or get allowlisted.

Activation questions#

  • "Awaiting payment" won't clear. Access flips to active only after the backend confirms Telegram Stars payment. If you paid and it hasn't updated, re-check status; run /subscribe again in the bot if the invoice expired.
  • Telegram link code expired. Generate a fresh link from MyFenrir and re-run the /start link_… handoff.
  • Asking for a card? The live rail is Telegram Stars — Fenrir won't ask for card details for it. Stripe card billing is a later standby and shows as pending until configured.
Support

Frequently asked questions

The operator questions that come up before connecting a domain, Telegram group, or paid room.

Do customers need to move their registrar?+

No. Keep Porkbun, Dynadot, GoDaddy, Namecheap, or any registrar. Fenrir only asks you to point DNS to Cloudflare so Lock URLs get automatic certificates. You never transfer your domain, and Cloudflare is never required as your registrar.

What happens when a Telegram invite leaks?+

You rotate or revoke the raw Telegram invite. The public Fenrir URL stays exactly the same, so everyone who's supposed to be in keeps their access — only the leaked invite dies. That's the invite kill-switch.

Can Fenrir host non-Telegram links?+

Yes. The All Links vault tracks payment links, docs, booking links, support links, and live-room links in one connected place, and you choose what's shared or hidden. Live Rooms route Zoom, Meet, Teams, or Whereby through Fenrir first.

What if I don't have a domain?+

Start instantly on a managed Fenrir subdomain like customer.myfenrir.com — perfect for testing, previews, or small operators. Upgrade to a custom domain whenever you're ready via the DNS Wizard.

What does Cloudflare automate?+

Routing DNS through Cloudflare lets Fenrir verify ownership, route CNAMEs, and request SSL certificates automatically — so your Lock and room URLs are secure without manual certificate work.

How do payments work?+

Telegram Stars opens inside the official Telegram payment box and is the live path today. Stripe card billing is the professional route for Pro and Operator once its backend is configured; until then it's shown as pending and Fenrir offers Stars.

Is the login on the site fully solid yet?+

Honestly, not entirely. OAuth sign-in works, but the live app uses a session shared across .myfenrir.com, which can look like a "login loop" or a session that isn't yours. The dependable identity anchor is Link Telegram ID via the Fenrir bot. See Troubleshooting → Login loop.

What's the difference between a Lock and a Gate?+

A Telegram Lock is one stable link in front of a Telegram group with invite rotation. A Community Gate is a branded public page that verifies identity and applies your entry rules before routing people onward — often to a Lock. Locks protect links; Gates brand and govern the front door.
