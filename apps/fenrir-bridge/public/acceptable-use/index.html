MyFenrir rules

Acceptable Use

These rules apply to MyFenrir accounts, memberships, public links, and connected services.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Do not use MyFenrir for

  • Malware, phishing, deceptive redirects, stolen credentials, or unauthorized access.
  • Spam, fraud, impersonation, harassment, or unlawful content.
  • Misrepresenting ownership of a brand, community, domain, membership, or payment destination.
  • Evading the rules or safety controls of Telegram, Cloudflare, or another connected provider.

Your responsibilities

You must be authorized to manage any account, group, domain, link, or destination you connect. Protect administrator access and promptly revoke compromised invitations or links.

Enforcement

We may limit or suspend access that creates a security risk, abuse, fraud, spam, impersonation, payment dispute, or legal exposure. To report a concern, contact hola@myfenrir.com.
