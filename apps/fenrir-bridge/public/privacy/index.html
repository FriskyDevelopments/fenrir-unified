MyFenrir is an independent third-party service. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Telegram.

1. What this service does

MyFenrir is a community gatekeeper. It decides whether a person may join or remain in a Telegram group or channel operated by a community administrator, and it sells an optional paid membership called The Pack. To do that it necessarily processes information about you.

2. Age requirement

This service is for adults only. You must be 18 years or older (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) to use @Myfenrir_bot or any MyFenrir gate. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we learn that a minor has used the service, we delete their data and revoke access. Community administrators using MyFenrir are responsible for the lawfulness of their own communities.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active and you are using a gated community, and afterwards only for as long as we need it to answer appeals, resolve disputes, and meet our legal and tax obligations. Payment records are kept for the period required by United States tax law.

If you ask us to delete your data we will do so, except for records we are legally required to retain. Our infrastructure providers keep their own operational logs under their own policies, which we do not control.

6. International transfers

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. The service is administered remotely from Mexico, where the company's sole member resides. If you are located elsewhere, using this service means your data will be transferred to and processed in those countries.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or object to a particular use. Write to hola@myfenrir.com from the email address on your account, or send /support to the bot.

We handle these requests manually and will respond within 30 days. Deletion removes your account link, access decisions and verification records; we keep payment records for the 7 years described above, because we are legally required to. Deleting your data ends your access to any gated community.

8. How we protect it

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Incoming Telegram requests are authenticated with a shared secret before they are processed. Credentials are stored separately from application data and are rotated when we have reason to believe they are exposed. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and take remedial action promptly.

9. Automated decisions

Access to a community may be decided automatically, based on human verification, membership status and rules set by the community administrator. You can contest any decision by writing to hola@myfenrir.com, and a person will review it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, announce it through the bot. Continuing to use the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
